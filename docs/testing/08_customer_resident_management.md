# Customer/Resident Management — Black-Box QA Test Cases

This document covers Resident ("Penghuni") master-data management and its admin-side 365°-view tabs that are in scope here (Summary, Receipts list, Send Receipt, Activity Log), admin management of Resident Documents (`resident-documents.*`, both nested under a Resident and under a Unit), Unit master-data management (CRUD, Convert Property, a note on the Unit Installments link), Unit Occupants and Unit Vehicles CRUD, and the Resident/customer self-service experience — on the Web Resident Portal and the Android app — for viewing the resident's own dashboard/account, viewing and updating the resident's own profile, viewing the resident's own property/unit details, and the owner-only Tenant ("Penyewa") management flow (add tenant, set billing payer, remove tenant). Billing, payment, invoice/receipt viewing, complaints, maintenance requests, and notifications/emergency features are **out of scope** for this document and are only cross-referenced briefly where the same page/tab also surfaces them; their full test cases live in the Billing & Payment, Complaints & Maintenance, and Notifications test-case documents respectively.

> **Note on "Request Sewakan Unit":** the research found no distinct, approval-gated "rental request" workflow anywhere in the current backend or frontend/mobile code. What actually exists is a direct, immediately-effective **Tenant Management** feature: a unit OWNER can add a tenant, designate who pays the bill (owner or tenant), and remove the tenant — all take effect immediately on submission, with no pending/approval state, no supervisor review, and no separate "request" record. This is fully implemented on both the Web Resident Portal (`TenantSettingsCard`, inside the Account tab hub) and the Android app (`property_screen.dart`, Card 4). All test cases below (module `TEN`) test this CURRENT direct-action behavior, not a request/approval flow.
