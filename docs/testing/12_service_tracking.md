# Service Tracking — Black-Box Test Cases

This document covers Duta Residence's field-collection and resident-service tracking surface: collector field visits (`CollectorVisitController`) with GPS check-in and photographic/GPS/signature evidence (`CollectorVisitEvidenceController`); payment promises / "janji bayar" (`PaymentPromiseController`); the resident complaint lifecycle from both the resident self-service portal (`ResidentPortalController`) and the admin/staff 365°-view (`ResidentDetailController`); the maintenance-request lifecycle from the same two sides, including the star rating; and dunning collection letters with server-generated PDFs (`CollectionLetterController`). All backend endpoints sit behind `auth:sanctum` plus the global `audit` middleware and Spatie permission gates; collector-role callers are additionally scoped via `CollectorAssignmentService::assertUnitAssigned()` on most (but, per the research, not provably all) mutating endpoints in this area. Two confirmed gaps recur throughout the module and are called out inline with `> Note:` callouts rather than treated as defects to "fix" in these test cases: (1) a Payment Promise's `status` field never transitions from `pending` to `broken` automatically — this is a 100% manual judgment call, with no scheduled job or listener anywhere in the codebase; and (2) collector-scoping (`assertUnitAssigned`) is explicitly documented for some read/write actions but is **not** documented as re-checked on Payment Promise updates, the visit-evidence list endpoint, or the collection-letter list/download endpoints — tests below probe this as *current behavior*, not as a presumed bug.

### 3. Payment Promises / Janji Bayar (PTP)

> Note: There is no automated job or scheduled command anywhere in the codebase that transitions a Payment Promise's `status` from `pending` to `broken` when `promised_date` passes. The only related automation is a "nearing due" notification (`promised_date <= today+2 days`, status still `pending`). The `status` field must be flipped to `broken` manually by a human via the generic update endpoint. The test cases below verify this current, manual-only behavior; they do not assume auto-detection exists.
